# Interview preparation

## A 45-second walkthrough

I built a Python platform to test whether adaptive strategy selection beats simpler allocation. Daily market data flows through validation, causal features, six strategies and either a rule, equal-weight or ML selector. The simulator executes at the following open with estimated costs and a liquidity cap. The biggest result was negative. The improved selector did not establish a material advantage over equal weighting. I can show the exact experiment, explain the failure mode and demonstrate the system without market credentials.

## Technical questions to prepare

**What goes into the model?** Momentum, market and relative returns, slope, trend strength, realized volatility, ATR, volume changes, RSI, deviation and breakout features, plus strategy identity and exposure scale. Signals are generated by each underlying strategy. The baseline gradient booster estimates cost-adjusted, volatility-penalized return from entry at the next open until the signal exits or five sessions elapse. The relative model learns deviations from the realized same-symbol/date mean where signals overlap. Its score is centered among current candidates and added at half weight to the absolute estimate. Scores are utility estimates, not probabilities.

**What was the feature bug?** The legacy one-hot assignment reused three continuous feature names. It silently overwrote momentum, trend and breakout. The V3 relative component prefixes strategy identity columns, preserving continuous features. The comparison retains the old mapping so the original experiment remains reproducible. This is also why I would inspect a feature matrix and test its schema, rather than trust estimator output alone.

**How did you prevent look-ahead?** Decisions use completed bars, simulation fills at the following open, and outcome timestamps are stored with each training row. Walk-forward folds exclude labels whose outcomes cross the boundary. Evaluation and deployment fit separately. The 2026 period became inspected when its preliminary diagnostics helped uncover the feature defect. A genuinely untouched confirmatory period is still required.

**Why didn’t the more complicated model win?** V2 achieved 50% within-date ranking accuracy across unequal realized pairs on the 2026 block. The ensemble changed portfolio returns very little and operated at much lower exposure than equal weighting. The paired interval was wide. I would retain equal weighting as the primary benchmark and test a locked future period before claiming a selection benefit.

**What would you change before operational deployment?** Validate actual routing and fills in an explicitly approved paper trial, including reconciliation across restarts and partial fills. Quantify auction and market-impact costs, add point-in-time membership and delisting treatment, and evaluate multiple locked periods. The research simulator, synthetic demo, paper adapter and independent risk gate have distinct responsibilities.
